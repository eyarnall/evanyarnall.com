I am a PhD candidate in Rehabilitation Science at Boston University Sargent College, working in the
[Development, Experience, and Motor Recovery (DEMR) Lab](https://sites.bu.edu/demrlab/) with
[Dr. Claudio Ferre](https://www.bu.edu/sargent/profile/claudio-ferre/). I study sensorimotor
integration in children with unilateral cerebral palsy, combining fNIRS and TMS to look at corticospinal
connectivity and sensory cortical responses while children reach.

My dissertation follows typically developing infants and infants at risk for cerebral palsy from 6 to 24
months to understand how sensorimotor development unfolds. With BU Engineering, I am also building a
moving-target reaching board that uses a physical reaching task for a more accurate, ecologically valid test
of sensorimotor integration.

Before the PhD, I coordinated clinical trials at Shriners Hospitals for Children, and at Boston University
School of Medicine I used wearable technology and behavioral coding to measure behavior. I like working across the whole research pipeline: designing apparatus and paradigms,
coding and analyzing behavior, and writing grants and manuscripts. I am also one of the inaugural
[PACES Scholars](https://www.precise-rehab.org/resources/paces) at the Precise Center, Johns Hopkins Medicine, a
program for early-stage investigators in precision rehabilitation, and I completed USC's NIH-funded
[ReproRehab](https://www.reprorehab.usc.edu/) program in reproducible data science in 2024–2025.

Outside the lab, I have volunteered with [CP Soccer New England](https://www.cpsoccer.us/) since spring 2024. It runs
soccer programs for kids with cerebral palsy and other neurological conditions. I also help staff the DEMR Lab's
[outreach booths](https://sites.bu.edu/demrlab/news/) at community events like Science on State Street, Open Streets
Boston, and Play to Thrive, where families try hands-on brain and movement activities.
